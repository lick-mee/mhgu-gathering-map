Gathering Map - gathering spots on the quest map for MHGU Recomp

Shows every gathering spot (mining, bone, bug, herb, mushroom, honey, berry, web...) on the quest maps with the
icon and color of the item it mostly gives.

Install
  Put this folder (zzz-gather-map) in the recomp's mods folder and start the game.

Options
  Press Insert in game and open "Gathering Map": switch it off, change the icon size, or also show other pickups
  (crates, barrels, ammo).
  Without a Map item the full map only shows the part around you, and so do the gathering points (on by default;
  "Visible radius without a map" sets how far, 40 by default). With a Map you see them all.
  Your choices are saved (gather_map.ini in the game's save folder) and kept at the next start.

Uninstall: delete this folder.
